# Operating Standards (Public)

These are the standards we publish so clients and partners know what to expect.
They exist to protect **systems**, **users**, and **clients** — and to keep all work **scope-respecting**.

## 1) Evidence & reproducibility
- We prioritize **reproducible behavior**, clear impact, and verification steps.
- Claims are tied to observable evidence and testable reasoning.

## 2) Scope, safety, and non-disruption
- We operate **within scope** and avoid production exploitation.
- No behavior intended to harm availability, integrity, or confidentiality.

## 3) Redaction policy (public-safe by default)
- Public writeups are sanitized: **no secrets**, **no exploit steps**, **no instructions that enable abuse**.
- We focus on outcomes, risk reduction, and mitigations.

## 4) Engineering-first delivery
- Changes are **surgical**, review-friendly, and tested when applicable.
- Security work is tied to reliability, release safety, and operational robustness.

## 5) Communication
- Clear, predictable updates.
- Professional tone, minimal noise, high signal.
